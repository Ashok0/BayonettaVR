Likely defect to investigate before attempting any replacement:
RecoverGameCamera checks orthogonality but not determinant. Orthogonal matrices
can include a reflection when recovering a view with a different handedness.
LevelPose always constructs a determinant +1 basis. If the recovered camera
has determinant -1, Correction therefore introduces a reflection. For example,
camera orientation diag(1,1,-1) becomes level diag(-1,1,-1), yielding correction
diag(-1,1,1). This mirrors X and reverses triangle winding, consistent with
the reported reversed movement and geometry vanishing through face culling.
The .55 tests generated only determinant +1 cameras and missed this case.
This explains a concrete flaw in the implementation; the actual live camera
determinant was not captured, so it is not yet a confirmed runtime root cause.

Do not redeploy .55. Future calibration requires live camera/view convention
verification and tests including reflected coordinate bases and triangle winding.
The rollback restores pre-calibration behavior; full floor calibration is withdrawn.
